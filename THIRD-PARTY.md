# Third-party components

Cinder Ash 0.01 is a separate theme by MisterKnot, not an Ambinance update.

- Kvantum SVG/configuration topology derives from **KvFlatRed**, installed Kvantum
  1.1.5, by Pedram Pourang (Tsu Jan), GPL-3.0-or-later.
  Source: https://github.com/tsujan/Kvantum/tree/master/Kvantum/themes/kvthemes/KvFlatRed
  Changes: all theme colors, explicit palette and text roles, toolbar surfaces,
  metadata, and selected rendering options.
- Plasma widget/frame geometry derives from the installed KDE Breeze assets,
  plasma-desktoptheme 6.6.6, LGPL-2.0-or-later. Original authors and notices are
  reproduced in LICENSES/Plasma-copyright.txt.
  Source: https://invent.kde.org/plasma/libplasma/-/tree/master/src/desktoptheme/breeze
  Changes: color literals, semantic color roles, theme metadata, and colors file.
- Wallpaper, Aurorae artwork, splash, SDDM UI, and installer scripts are original
  Cinder Ash work by MisterKnot, GPL-3.0-or-later. License texts are in LICENSES.

No icon or cursor theme is bundled. Standard window-control glyphs are part of
the window decoration, not an icon-theme replacement.
